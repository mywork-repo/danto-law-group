About the Firm

Navigating the Skies of Justice

Danto Law Group is a specialized aviation law firm based in Jakarta, Indonesia. Led by Columbanus Priaardanto, S.H., the firm provides deep expertise in aviation law, criminal procedure, and international air regulations.

Columbanus Priaardanto has handled landmark cases including the Sriwijaya Air SJ182 accident litigation. He combines academic rigor — evidenced by multiple published legal works — with sharp courtroom advocacy that protects his clients' interests at the highest level.
